Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Paris, France

The city of lights is famous for the Eiffel Tower, art museums, cafes, and beautiful riverside walks.

"A dream come true. Every street felt like a postcard."

- Review by Riya

2. Goa, India

Goa offers golden beaches, lively markets, Portuguese-style architecture, and fresh seafood.

"Perfect place to relax with friends and family."

- Review by Karan

3. Manali, India

Snow-covered mountains, pine forests, and adventure sports make Manali a hill station favourite.

"The mountain views are unforgettable."

- Review by Neha

Travel Tips

  • Book tickets and hotels early.
  • Carry a valid photo ID.
  • Pack according to the weather.
  • Keep digital and paper copies of documents.
